Checkers

Two players, one screen. Diagonal moves, jump to capture.

How to play: Red moves first. Tap a piece, then tap a diagonal square to move it, or jump over an opponent's piece to capture it. Reach the far row to crown a king, which can then move diagonally in either direction.

Common Questions

Is this checkers game free to play?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up, no account, and no downloads required.

Can I play checkers against a friend?

Yes — this is a local two-player game, meaning you and a friend take turns on the same screen and device.

Is there a computer opponent?

Not currently — this version is built for two people playing together on the same device.

Are forced jumps enabled?

No — you can choose any legal move, even if a jump is available, so beginners aren't punished for missing a capture.
