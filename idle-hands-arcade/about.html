About Idle Hands Arcade

Idle Hands Arcade is a small, free collection of browser games built for the in-between moments — waiting on a bus, a slow afternoon at the office, a few spare minutes before a call starts. No accounts, no downloads, no fine print to click through. Just open a page and play.

Right now that's four games: a word scramble, a classic 9x9 sudoku with three difficulty levels, a 15-tile sliding puzzle, and local two-player checkers. Each one keeps a simple scoreboard in your browser, so your best runs are still there the next time you drop by.

The site is intentionally simple: no logins, no data sold, no clutter. It's built and maintained independently, and new games get added from time to time.

Have a question, a bug to report, or a game you'd like to see added? Get in touch on the contact page.
