Word Scramble

Unscramble the letters. Press Enter or tap Check.

How to play: The letters of a hidden word are shuffled above. Use the hint to help you guess, type your answer, and hit Check (or Enter). Stuck? Skip to a new word any time.

Word scrambles have been a staple of newspaper puzzle pages for generations, prized because they're quick enough for a coffee break but tricky enough to actually make you think. This version pulls from a rotating list of everyday words, each with a short hint, so you can play a few rounds back to back without seeing the same word twice in a row.
