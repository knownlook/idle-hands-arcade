Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Idle Hands Arcade ("we," "this site") is a collection of free browser games. This page explains what data is collected and how it's used.

Information we collect

The games on this site keep score using your browser's local storage (for example, your solved-word count or best puzzle time). This information stays in your browser only — it is not sent to us, not linked to your identity, and not shared with anyone. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

Cookies and advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at Google's Partner Sites policy, and opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Analytics

We may use an analytics tool (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall traffic patterns — for example, which games are most played. This data is aggregated and not used to identify individual visitors.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The games are freely playable without any account or personal information required.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out via the contact page.
